# 04. Fair Use for News Reporting — সংবাদ প্রচারের উদ্দেশ্যে ফেয়ার ইউজ

**Use for:** News reporting, current affairs, public-interest journalism, commentary, criticism and reporting on events.

### Submission Text

Our video was created and published for genuine news reporting, public information and journalistic purposes.

The claimed material was incorporated into our news report to provide relevant context, illustrate the reported event and inform viewers about matters of public interest.

Our video contains original editorial work, including news presentation, narration, reporting, editing, commentary and contextual information.

The copyrighted material was used only to the extent considered necessary for the reporting purpose and was not intended to replace, reproduce or serve as a substitute for the original copyrighted work.

Our purpose is to inform the public about the relevant news event, not to redistribute the original material as entertainment or as a replacement for its original publication.

We respectfully request that the claim be reviewed in light of the journalistic purpose, editorial context and applicable copyright exceptions, including fair use or fair dealing where applicable.

**News Organization:** [CHANNEL / MEDIA HOUSE NAME]  
**Video Title:** [VIDEO TITLE]  
**Original Source:** [SOURCE NAME]

The material identified in the removal request was either independently created, properly authorized for use, incorrectly identified or used under a legally applicable copyright exception.

I respectfully request that the removal request be reconsidered in light of the original production evidence, authorization documents and the actual context of the material.

I am willing to provide relevant supporting evidence to establish the legitimate basis for the use of the content.

**Removed Video:** [VIDEO TITLE]  
**Video URL:** [REMOVED VIDEO URL]  
**Copyright Claimant:** [CLAIMANT NAME]  
**Removal Reference:** [REFERENCE NUMBER]  
**Supporting Evidence:** [EVIDENCE LINKS]

### Important Legal Requirements

A YouTube counter notification is a formal legal process, not an ordinary copyright appeal.

The official process requires the uploader's full legal name, physical address, telephone number, a specific statement explaining the mistake or misidentification, required legal declarations and a valid signature.

Do not submit this template without verifying that you meet the legal requirements and genuinely believe the removal was incorrect.

# 15. Important Copyright Compliance Notes — গুরুত্বপূর্ণ কপিরাইট নির্দেশনা

1. Giving credit to the original creator does not automatically grant permission to use copyrighted material.

2. Publicly accessible content is not automatically public-domain content.

3. News reporting does not automatically qualify every use of copyrighted footage or music as fair use.

4. Background music captured unintentionally can still be copyrighted.

5. A valid license must cover the relevant platform, usage type, monetization, territory and duration where those restrictions apply.

6. Do not claim original ownership of third-party footage, music or recordings.

7. Do not submit a copyright dispute simply because a video is not monetized or because the content is being used for educational purposes.

8. A copyright claim, copyright strike and Community Guidelines strike are different enforcement actions and require different resolution processes.

9. Always retain original footage, project files, license certificates, permission letters and source URLs.

10. Never submit inaccurate ownership declarations, fabricated licenses or misleading legal statements.
